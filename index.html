The Gift of Testimony

By Elder Gérald Caussé

Gift

  • a notable capacity, talent, or endowment
  • something voluntarily transferred by one person to another without compensation
  • the act, right, or power of giving

Testimony: Word Origin

testimonium Latin — evidence, witness

testis Latin — witness

English testimony comes from Latin testimonium, “evidence, witness,” from testis, “witness.”

In Late Latin, testimonium was also used for the Decalogue — the Ten Commandments as a written testimony.

  • How has giving or receiving a gift strengthened your relationship with someone?
  • When have you spoken up as a witness to help someone understand what really happened? What made it easy or difficult to tell the truth?

A testimony is a rare type of gift.

It is a gift from God to us.

It is also a gift from ourselves, to ourselves, and we share it.

It is also, and finally, it is a gift to those around us.

When others share their testimony, the divine part of our nature wants to absorb the truth and cause our own testimony to grow stronger.

Satan will try to fan the flames of our human nature, to cause us to compare our testimony to others, to think "i wish my testimony were like that" or similar thoughts

What does the Lord expect me to do with this gift?
